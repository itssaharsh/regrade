- What it does: paste results → bands by goals per game, each division above counted as 2 goals a game stronger (drag or arrows; every moved team shows its old division) → set venue names, pitches, kick-off times and the first Saturday → generate four weeks (avoids repeat pairings, home/away within one, one fixture per slot, odd bands rest one team a week in turn; relaxations are counted and explained) → fixtures that don't fit are listed → download fixtureupload.csv in the Full-Time uploader's nine-column layout.
- What's real: parsing, banding, scheduler, export, tests, validate script. What's synthetic: the sample league (24 fictional clubs, 48 teams, six Saturdays of generated results), labelled on screen. Uploader layout from the FA's 2015 guide; confirm against your uploader.
- Numbers you can quote from the app on the sample: 144 results, 48 teams, 3 bands of 16, 24 slots per Saturday, 96 fixtures in four weeks, 0 clashes, 0 repeat pairings, 18 teams change division; bands place 16 of 48 teams away from their true strength against 24 for the league's guess (validate); with two pitches removed: 18 slots, 24 fixtures don't fit and are listed.
- Lessons you might write about (in your words): the first pairing algorithm rotated a fixed round robin and could not avoid pairings the old divisions had played (4 repeats) and mis-balanced home/away (gap 4); the validate script caught it before any UI existed; the fix was a per-week matching search (docs/adr/0002).
